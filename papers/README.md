# Papers

My reading notes and reimplementations of research papers, one folder per paper
(`<year>_<short_title>/`), each started from `sample.md`.

## Respecting others' work

The papers here are the work of their authors, and we respect that work and the terms it is
shared under:

- **PDFs stay local.** Paper files are kept on my machine only and are never committed
  (`papers/**/*.pdf` is git-ignored). Redistributing a copy isn't ours to do, even when the paper
  is free to read.
- **We share links, not copies.** Each `notes.md` links to the official source (arXiv, DOI, the
  publisher or the authors' page), so readers get the paper from where its authors put it.
- **Credit is always given.** Every note names the authors and year, and reimplementations say
  which paper they reproduce.
- **Attribution isn't permission.** Figures and tables are not copied: link to them or redraw them
  from scratch. Quotes are kept short and cited. Content is reused as-is only when the paper's
  license allows it (e.g. CC BY).
- **Licenses are followed.** Before reusing official code or data, check its license and keep its
  notice.
