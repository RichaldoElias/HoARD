Read in three passes, following S. Keshav, "How to Read a Paper" (ACM SIGCOMM Computer
Communication Review, 2007). Stop after any pass if the paper isn't worth more time.

## Pass 1: the five Cs (5–10 minutes)

Read the title, abstract, introduction, section headings and conclusion only.

- **Category:** what kind of paper is it? (new method, analysis, benchmark, survey...)
- **Context:** which earlier work does it build on?
- **Correctness:** do the assumptions look valid?
- **Contributions:** what is new?
- **Clarity:** is it well written?
