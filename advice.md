# Advice

## Learning

> "What I cannot create, I do not understand."
>
> — Richard Feynman, written on his Caltech blackboard at the time of his death (1988). Caltech Archives.

### Exercises: struggle first, then check

1. **Give each exercise at least 30–60 minutes of real effort** before looking at the solution. Getting stuck is part of the learning, not a sign it isn't working.
2. **Then check the solution** and compare it with your attempt.
3. **Write down what you missed:** the concept, trick, or API you didn't know or didn't think of.
4. **Write down what to do differently** next time: the habit, check, or approach that would have gotten you there.
